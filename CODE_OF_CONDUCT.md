# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Everyone participating in Legacy Clicker discussions, issues, and pull requests must be respectful and constructive.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- Giving useful, specific feedback
- Respecting different experience levels and viewpoints
- Keeping discussions focused on the project
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or sexualized language
- Publishing another person's private information
- Spam, deliberate disruption, or impersonation

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct problems privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.
